Restaurant Services

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest spaces to keep comfortable. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods pull conditioned air outside nonstop, and the makeup air system has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. Brentwood HVAC sizes, repairs, and maintains the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep both the kitchen and the dining room comfortable, and we schedule service around your operating hours.

Many restaurant comfort complaints trace back to air balance. When a kitchen runs negative, it pulls dining-room air the wrong way, sucks hot or cold outdoor air through every door, and wears out your staff during the rush. We balance makeup air against hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports that landlords and auditors can review.

We work around your business, not the other way around. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. When the work can't happen during service, we install after hours, and disruption stays minimal so the kitchen keeps cooking and the dining room stays comfortable.

Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Brentwood HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Consistent professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We maintain service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or auditor asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Rapid Response

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Brentwood

Every hour without climate control costs a business money. In a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. On a retail floor, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. In a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it leads to tenant disputes. Brentwood HVAC provides fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties across Brentwood and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. The cause could be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, an ignition or gas valve fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board lockout. Whatever it is, we diagnose it fast, restore heating or cooling, and record every step for your service records. Afterward, we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.
